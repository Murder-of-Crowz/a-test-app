### Full Description

Esthi helps esthetician students study for state board exams with focused practice questions, flashcards, quizzes, and exam-style review.

Study by topic, review missed questions, track your progress, and build confidence with practice modes designed around common esthetics exam categories:

- Safety and infection control
- Skin care
- Skin analysis
- Hair removal
- Advanced treatments
- Makeup
- Client consultation

Free users can study with a 200-question bank. Esthi Pro unlocks the full 1,400-question bank, premium English and Spanish question sets, and unlimited mock exam access.

Features:

- Flashcards with Know It and Still Learning ratings
- Topic quizzes with randomized answer order
- Weighted 25-question practice exams
- 100-question timed mock exams for Esthi Pro users
- Progress tracking for flashcards, quizzes, and exams
- Missed-question review
- English and Spanish study modes
- Optional daily study reminders
- Local-first study progress storage

Esthi is an educational study aid. It does not guarantee passing any exam, certification, license, state board requirement, or other professional requirement. Requirements may vary by state, jurisdiction, school, and testing provider.
